How We Close

Four steps to a clean closing

From contract to keys, we keep agents, lenders, and clients informed — with RESPA compliance and TRID guidelines as non‑negotiables.

  1. 1

    Open the file

    Send the contract and party details. We confirm receipt, open the file, and outline what each side needs for a smooth path to closing.

  2. 2

    Title examination

    We research title, run lien searches, and flag issues early so they can be cured before anyone is sitting at the table.

  3. 3

    Documents & clear to close

    We prepare closing documents, coordinate with lenders, and educate buyers and sellers so there are no surprises on signing day.

  4. 4

    Closing day

    Accurate numbers, professional hosting, and post-closing follow-through — so everyone leaves confident the deal is done right.
