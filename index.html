FAQ

Questions taxpayers actually ask

Call either office if you need a faster answer — we’ll treat it as confidential case intake.

Can you stop an IRS wage garnishment?

Yes. We move quickly to contact the IRS, request releases when appropriate, and set up a resolution path so enforcement can pause while your case is worked.

Can you stop levies or seizures?

Yes. During investigation we develop a plan and work to ensure that any garnishments, levies, or seizures are stopped immediately while we establish contact with the IRS on your behalf.

Who leads Geaux Tax Resolution?

Geaux Tax Resolution is led by former IRS employee Bryon Garrety. Bryon and his team have over 35 years of experience dealing directly with the IRS.

Will my call stay confidential?

Yes. Tax problems are personal. We treat every conversation as confidential case intake, not a sales script.

What should I have ready for the first call?

Any IRS letters, a rough sense of the balance, and notes on unfiled years or prior payment plans. Photos of the notice are fine for a first call.
